# ASTERA Project Brief

## Brand
**ASTERA**

**Tagline:** Exploring New Brands. Delivering Quality.

## Business
ASTERA is a local mobile and technology accessories shop being developed into a recognizable consumer-tech brand.

The current store sells products such as:
- charging cables
- chargers and power accessories
- earphones and earbuds
- speakers
- mobile cases
- screen protectors
- adapters
- other everyday mobile accessories

## Brand mission
Discover promising new, emerging, and local accessory brands and make their quality products easier for customers to discover and buy.

## Brand promise
ASTERA helps customers discover useful technology accessories without making brand popularity the only measure of value.

## Target customers
Primary:
- students
- young professionals
- everyday smartphone users
- customers looking for affordable accessories
- customers interested in discovering alternatives to large brands

Secondary:
- local/emerging accessory brands
- suppliers and distributors
- future online customers

## Website goals
1. Establish ASTERA as a credible brand.
2. Show what ASTERA sells.
3. Highlight emerging/local brands.
4. Communicate the quality-first philosophy.
5. Make products easy to discover.
6. Provide clear contact/location information.
7. Create a foundation for future online commerce.

## Initial website sections
- Home
- Shop / Products
- Brands
- Categories
- About ASTERA
- Quality / Why ASTERA
- Contact / Visit Us

Future:
- Product detail pages
- Online ordering
- Reviews
- Brand onboarding
- Offers
- Blog / discovery stories

## Core homepage message
### Discover What's New.
**Exploring New Brands. Delivering Quality.**

ASTERA brings together mobile and tech accessories from emerging and local brands, selected with a focus on everyday usefulness and quality.

## Brand story
ASTERA is inspired by the idea of exploration: the universe contains countless stars, but only some become visible to us.

In the same spirit, ASTERA looks beyond the biggest names to discover smaller and emerging brands with products worth knowing.

## Content principles
Say:
- emerging brands
- local brands
- new brands
- carefully selected
- quality-focused
- everyday technology
- discover something new

Avoid:
- "cheap"
- "unknown brands"
- "minor brands"
- unsupported superlatives
- fake certifications
- exaggerated quality claims

## Success criteria
A visitor should understand within 5 seconds:
1. ASTERA is a technology/mobile accessories brand.
2. ASTERA discovers new/emerging brands.
3. ASTERA focuses on quality.
4. The visitor can explore products or visit/contact the store.
